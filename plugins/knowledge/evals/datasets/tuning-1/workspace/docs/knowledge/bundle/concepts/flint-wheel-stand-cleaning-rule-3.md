# Flint wheel stand cleaning rule 3

Cleaning rule 3 applies to the handles of the Flint wheel stand.

Use a damp cloth, then dry the surface before storage.

Do not apply this rule to lenses, wiring, bearings, or sealed housings.

Keep the Flint wheel stand clear of the marked aisle by at least 22 centimetres.
