# Brass pump bench label scheme 7

Label scheme 7 uses a green triangle on the case of the Brass pump bench.

The triangle marks the approved carrying side, not inspection status.

An unreadable triangle must be replaced before the case leaves storage.

Keep the Brass pump bench clear of the marked aisle by at least 26 centimetres.
