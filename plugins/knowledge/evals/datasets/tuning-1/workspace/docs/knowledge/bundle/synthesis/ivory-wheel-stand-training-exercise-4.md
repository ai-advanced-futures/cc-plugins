# Ivory wheel stand training exercise 4

Training exercise 4 teaches safe handling of the Ivory wheel stand.

The exercise takes 75 minutes in the parts loft.

A trainee must identify the lifting points and show the stop signal before passing.

Keep the Ivory wheel stand clear of the marked aisle by at least 23 centimetres.
