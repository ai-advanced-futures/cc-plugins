# Dew soil sieve training exercise 12

Training exercise 12 teaches safe handling of the Dew soil sieve.

The exercise takes 33 minutes in the tool porch.

A trainee must identify the lifting points and show the stop signal before passing.

The handling team for the Dew soil sieve must include 4 trained operators.
