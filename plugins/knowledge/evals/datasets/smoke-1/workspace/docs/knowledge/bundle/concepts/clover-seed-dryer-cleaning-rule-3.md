# Clover seed dryer cleaning rule 3

Cleaning rule 3 applies to the handles of the Clover seed dryer.

Use a damp cloth, then dry the surface before storage.

Do not apply this rule to lenses, wiring, bearings, or sealed housings.

Keep the Clover seed dryer clear of the marked aisle by at least 22 centimetres.
