Training exercise 4 teaches safe handling of the Dew seed dryer.

The exercise takes 25 minutes in the potting shed.

A trainee must identify the lifting points and show the stop signal before passing.

Keep the Dew seed dryer clear of the marked aisle by at least 23 centimetres.
