Storage bin 10 in the potting shed holds adapters for the Birch soil sieve.

The seed keeper checks the bin label at the start of each month.

The bin has room for 33 adapters; damaged adapters go in a separate red box.

The handling team for the Birch soil sieve must include 2 trained operators.
