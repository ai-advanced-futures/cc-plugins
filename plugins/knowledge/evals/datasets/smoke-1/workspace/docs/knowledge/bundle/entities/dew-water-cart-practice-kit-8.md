# Dew water cart practice kit 8

Practice kit 8 for the Dew water cart contains 32 wooden spacers.

The kit stays in the north glasshouse and the water steward signs it out.

The wooden spacers are for training only and must not be installed on working equipment.

Keep the Dew water cart clear of the marked aisle by at least 27 centimetres.
