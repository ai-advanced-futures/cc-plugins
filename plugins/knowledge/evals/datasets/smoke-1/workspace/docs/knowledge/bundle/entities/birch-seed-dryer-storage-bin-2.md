# Birch seed dryer storage bin 2

Storage bin 2 in the north glasshouse holds adapters for the Birch seed dryer.

The water steward checks the bin label at the start of each month.

The bin has room for 25 adapters; damaged adapters go in a separate red box.

Keep the Birch seed dryer clear of the marked aisle by at least 21 centimetres.
