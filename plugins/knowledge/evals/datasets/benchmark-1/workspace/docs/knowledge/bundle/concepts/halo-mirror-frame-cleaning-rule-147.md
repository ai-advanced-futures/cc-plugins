# Halo mirror frame cleaning rule 147

Cleaning rule 147 applies to the handles of the Halo mirror frame.

Use a damp cloth, then dry the surface before storage.

Do not apply this rule to lenses, wiring, bearings, or sealed housings.

Use tray 4 for loose parts removed from the Halo mirror frame.
