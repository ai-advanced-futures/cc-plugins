# Halo lens carrier cleaning rule 67

Cleaning rule 67 applies to the handles of the Halo lens carrier.

Use a damp cloth, then dry the surface before storage.

Do not apply this rule to lenses, wiring, bearings, or sealed housings.

Use tray 1 for loose parts removed from the Halo lens carrier.
