# Aster shutter drive cleaning rule 91

Cleaning rule 91 applies to the handles of the Aster shutter drive.

Use a damp cloth, then dry the surface before storage.

Do not apply this rule to lenses, wiring, bearings, or sealed housings.

The handling team for the Aster shutter drive must include 2 trained operators.
