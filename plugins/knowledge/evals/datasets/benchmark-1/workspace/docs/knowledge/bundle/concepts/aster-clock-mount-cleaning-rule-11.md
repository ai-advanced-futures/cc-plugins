# Aster clock mount cleaning rule 11

Cleaning rule 11 applies to the handles of the Aster clock mount.

Use a damp cloth, then dry the surface before storage.

Do not apply this rule to lenses, wiring, bearings, or sealed housings.

The handling team for the Aster clock mount must include 3 trained operators.
