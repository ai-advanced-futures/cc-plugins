# Comet lens carrier cleaning rule 3

Cleaning rule 3 applies to the handles of the Comet lens carrier.

Use a damp cloth, then dry the surface before storage.

Do not apply this rule to lenses, wiring, bearings, or sealed housings.

Keep the Comet lens carrier clear of the marked aisle by at least 22 centimetres.
