# Comet sensor tray cleaning rule 43

Cleaning rule 43 applies to the handles of the Comet sensor tray.

Use a damp cloth, then dry the surface before storage.

Do not apply this rule to lenses, wiring, bearings, or sealed housings.

Keep the Comet sensor tray clear of the marked aisle by at least 20 centimetres.
