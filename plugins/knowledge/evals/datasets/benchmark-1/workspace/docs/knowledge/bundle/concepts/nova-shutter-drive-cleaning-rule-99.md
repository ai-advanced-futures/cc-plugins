# Nova shutter drive cleaning rule 99

Cleaning rule 99 applies to the handles of the Nova shutter drive.

Use a damp cloth, then dry the surface before storage.

Do not apply this rule to lenses, wiring, bearings, or sealed housings.

Record the Nova shutter drive case temperature after 18 minutes indoors.
