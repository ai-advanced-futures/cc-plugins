# Comet clock mount packing trial 73

Packing trial 73 used 202 paper pads around the Comet clock mount.

The pads stopped side movement but did not stop vertical movement.

The trial team recommended a top brace before the next transport test.

Keep the Comet clock mount at least 45 centimetres from the drying rack.
