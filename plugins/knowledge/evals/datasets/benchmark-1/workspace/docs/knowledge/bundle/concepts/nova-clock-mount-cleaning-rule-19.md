# Nova clock mount cleaning rule 19

Cleaning rule 19 applies to the handles of the Nova clock mount.

Use a damp cloth, then dry the surface before storage.

Do not apply this rule to lenses, wiring, bearings, or sealed housings.

Record the Nova clock mount case temperature after 6 minutes indoors.
