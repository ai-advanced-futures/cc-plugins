# Aster shutter drive label scheme 151

Label scheme 151 uses a green triangle on the case of the Aster shutter drive.

The triangle marks the approved carrying side, not inspection status.

An unreadable triangle must be replaced before the case leaves storage.

Use tray 8 for loose parts removed from the Aster shutter drive.
