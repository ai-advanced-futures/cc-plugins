# Beacon filter drum training exercise 52

Training exercise 52 teaches safe handling of the Beacon filter drum.

The exercise takes 183 minutes in the dome store.

A trainee must identify the lifting points and show the stop signal before passing.

The handling team for the Beacon filter drum must include 2 trained operators.
