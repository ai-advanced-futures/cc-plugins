# Halo sensor tray label scheme 47

Label scheme 47 uses a green triangle on the case of the Halo sensor tray.

The triangle marks the approved carrying side, not inspection status.

An unreadable triangle must be replaced before the case leaves storage.

Keep the Halo sensor tray clear of the marked aisle by at least 24 centimetres.
