# Comet mirror frame cleaning rule 83

Cleaning rule 83 applies to the handles of the Comet mirror frame.

Use a damp cloth, then dry the surface before storage.

Do not apply this rule to lenses, wiring, bearings, or sealed housings.

Keep the Comet mirror frame clear of the marked aisle by at least 39 centimetres.
