# Ember shutter drive cleaning rule 155

Cleaning rule 155 applies to the handles of the Ember shutter drive.

Use a damp cloth, then dry the surface before storage.

Do not apply this rule to lenses, wiring, bearings, or sealed housings.

Keep the Ember shutter drive at least 32 centimetres from the drying rack.
