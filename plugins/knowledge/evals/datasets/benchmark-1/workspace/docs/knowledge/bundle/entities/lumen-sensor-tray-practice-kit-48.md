# Lumen sensor tray practice kit 48

Practice kit 48 for the Lumen sensor tray contains 182 wooden spacers.

The kit stays in the east annex and the instrument checker signs it out.

The wooden spacers are for training only and must not be installed on working equipment.

Keep the Lumen sensor tray clear of the marked aisle by at least 25 centimetres.
