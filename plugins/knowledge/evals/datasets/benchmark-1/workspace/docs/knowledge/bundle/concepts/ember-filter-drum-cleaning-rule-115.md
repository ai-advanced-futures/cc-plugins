# Ember filter drum cleaning rule 115

Cleaning rule 115 applies to the handles of the Ember filter drum.

Use a damp cloth, then dry the surface before storage.

Do not apply this rule to lenses, wiring, bearings, or sealed housings.

Keep the Ember filter drum at least 30 centimetres from the drying rack.
