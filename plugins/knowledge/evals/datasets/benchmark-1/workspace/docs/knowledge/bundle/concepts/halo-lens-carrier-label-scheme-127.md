# Halo lens carrier label scheme 127

Label scheme 127 uses a green triangle on the case of the Halo lens carrier.

The triangle marks the approved carrying side, not inspection status.

An unreadable triangle must be replaced before the case leaves storage.

Keep the Halo lens carrier clear of the marked aisle by at least 20 centimetres.
