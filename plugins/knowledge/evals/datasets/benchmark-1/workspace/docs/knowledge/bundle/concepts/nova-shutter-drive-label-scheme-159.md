# Nova shutter drive label scheme 159

Label scheme 159 uses a green triangle on the case of the Nova shutter drive.

The triangle marks the approved carrying side, not inspection status.

An unreadable triangle must be replaced before the case leaves storage.

Keep the Nova shutter drive at least 36 centimetres from the drying rack.
