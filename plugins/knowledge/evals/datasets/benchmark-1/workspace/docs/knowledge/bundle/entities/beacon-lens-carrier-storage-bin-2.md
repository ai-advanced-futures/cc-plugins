# Beacon lens carrier storage bin 2

Storage bin 2 in the calibration room holds adapters for the Beacon lens carrier.

The optics custodian checks the bin label at the start of each month.

The bin has room for 135 adapters; damaged adapters go in a separate red box.

Keep the Beacon lens carrier clear of the marked aisle by at least 21 centimetres.
