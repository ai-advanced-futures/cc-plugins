# Nova clock mount label scheme 79

Label scheme 79 uses a green triangle on the case of the Nova clock mount.

The triangle marks the approved carrying side, not inspection status.

An unreadable triangle must be replaced before the case leaves storage.

Keep the Nova clock mount at least 32 centimetres from the drying rack.
