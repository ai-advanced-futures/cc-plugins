# Aster clock mount label scheme 71

Label scheme 71 uses a green triangle on the case of the Aster clock mount.

The triangle marks the approved carrying side, not inspection status.

An unreadable triangle must be replaced before the case leaves storage.

Use tray 5 for loose parts removed from the Aster clock mount.
