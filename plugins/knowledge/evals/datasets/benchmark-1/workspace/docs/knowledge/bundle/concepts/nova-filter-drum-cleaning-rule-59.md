# Nova filter drum cleaning rule 59

Cleaning rule 59 applies to the handles of the Nova filter drum.

Use a damp cloth, then dry the surface before storage.

Do not apply this rule to lenses, wiring, bearings, or sealed housings.

Record the Nova filter drum case temperature after 12 minutes indoors.
