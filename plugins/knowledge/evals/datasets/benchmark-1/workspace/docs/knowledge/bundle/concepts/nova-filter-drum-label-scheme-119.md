# Nova filter drum label scheme 119

Label scheme 119 uses a green triangle on the case of the Nova filter drum.

The triangle marks the approved carrying side, not inspection status.

An unreadable triangle must be replaced before the case leaves storage.

Keep the Nova filter drum at least 34 centimetres from the drying rack.
