# Ember clock mount cleaning rule 75

Cleaning rule 75 applies to the handles of the Ember clock mount.

Use a damp cloth, then dry the surface before storage.

Do not apply this rule to lenses, wiring, bearings, or sealed housings.

Keep the Ember clock mount at least 47 centimetres from the drying rack.
