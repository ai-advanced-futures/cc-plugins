# Drift mirror frame training exercise 84

Training exercise 84 teaches safe handling of the Drift mirror frame.

The exercise takes 215 minutes in the east annex.

A trainee must identify the lifting points and show the stop signal before passing.

Keep the Drift mirror frame clear of the marked aisle by at least 40 centimetres.
