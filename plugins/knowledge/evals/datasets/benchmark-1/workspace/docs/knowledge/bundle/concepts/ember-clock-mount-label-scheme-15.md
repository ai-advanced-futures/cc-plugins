# Ember clock mount label scheme 15

Label scheme 15 uses a green triangle on the case of the Ember clock mount.

The triangle marks the approved carrying side, not inspection status.

An unreadable triangle must be replaced before the case leaves storage.

The handling team for the Ember clock mount must include 4 trained operators.
