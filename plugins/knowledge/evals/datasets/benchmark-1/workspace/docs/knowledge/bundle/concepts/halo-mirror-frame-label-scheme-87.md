# Halo mirror frame label scheme 87

Label scheme 87 uses a green triangle on the case of the Halo mirror frame.

The triangle marks the approved carrying side, not inspection status.

An unreadable triangle must be replaced before the case leaves storage.

Keep the Halo mirror frame clear of the marked aisle by at least 22 centimetres.
