# Orbit shutter drive practice kit 160

Practice kit 160 for the Orbit shutter drive contains 294 wooden spacers.

The kit stays in the dome store and the night coordinator signs it out.

The wooden spacers are for training only and must not be installed on working equipment.

Keep the Orbit shutter drive at least 37 centimetres from the drying rack.
