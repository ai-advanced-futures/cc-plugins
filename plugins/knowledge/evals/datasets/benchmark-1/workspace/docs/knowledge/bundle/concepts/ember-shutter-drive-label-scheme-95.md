# Ember shutter drive label scheme 95

Label scheme 95 uses a green triangle on the case of the Ember shutter drive.

The triangle marks the approved carrying side, not inspection status.

An unreadable triangle must be replaced before the case leaves storage.

The handling team for the Ember shutter drive must include 3 trained operators.
