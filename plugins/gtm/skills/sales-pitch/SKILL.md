# Pitch Construction

## Purpose

Build a pitch that teaches, tailors, and takes control — not a pitch that features-dumps. The best pitch makes the buyer say "we've been thinking about this wrong."

## Pitch Architecture

### The Teaching Pitch (Challenger Sale — 5 Parts)

1. **Rational Drowning**: Show the buyer data that reframes their problem (something they didn't know)
2. **Emotional Impact**: Connect the reframe to their specific situation — make it personal
3. **Value Proposition**: Introduce your approach as the logical answer to the reframe
4. **New World**: Paint the picture of life after the problem is solved
5. **Your Product**: Now introduce the product as the vehicle to the New World

### StoryBrand Overlay
Map the SB7 structure onto the parts of the pitch:
- Parts 1-2: Character + Problem (the buyer's world today)
- Parts 3-4: Guide (why you, why now)
- Parts 5-6: Plan (3 steps to success)
- Part 7: CTA + Failure vs. Success contrast

## Truth Tier Gate
Every capability claim in every part receives a Truth Tier before the pitch is finalized.
- Part-level integrity score reported
- Tier 5 parts are rewritten before delivery

## Output
`pitch-deck.json` with the parts of the pitch, key points per part, truth tier per claim.
