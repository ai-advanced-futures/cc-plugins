# Brand Identity

## Purpose

Build the complete brand identity system before any campaign, launch, or sales material is produced. Every downstream artifact inherits the brand identity, so an error here repeats in all of them.

## Identity Build Sequence

Execute in this order (each informs the next):

### 1. The Why (Sinek Golden Circle)
Define the belief that drives the business.
- Why statement: "We believe [world view]"
- How principles: 3-5 distinctive methods
- What: the product that makes the How tangible

### 2. The Owned Word (Ries & Trout)
Name the single word you want to own in customer minds.
- Is this word available? No competitor owns it?
- If taken: can you create a new category?
- Validate: 6-word positioning line

### 3. The StoryBrand Script (Miller SB7)
- Character: who is the hero (ICP)
- Problem: external + internal + philosophical
- Guide: your brand (empathy + authority)
- Plan: 3 steps to solve the problem
- Call to Action: direct CTA
- Failure: what the customer loses by taking no action
- Success: the vision of winning

### 4. The Aaker Identity Grid
- Brand as Product (category, attributes, quality)
- Brand as Person (personality traits, customer relationship)
- Brand as Symbol (visual language, metaphor)
- Brand as Organization (values)
- Distill: core identity (2-3 timeless elements)

### 5. The Experience Positioning (Pine)
- Where on the progression: Commodity / Good / Service / Experience / Transformation?
- What transformation does the customer undergo?
- Design 2-3 experience staging elements

## Truth Tier Gate
All capability claims in brand artifacts must be Tier 1-2 before delivery.
Run the skill `integrity-audit` on all outputs.

## Outputs
- `brand-identity.json`
- `messaging-architecture.json`
- `voice-guide.md` (tone, phrases to use, vocabulary to avoid)
- `purpose-statement.md` (Why + How + What, 1 page)
- `storybrand-script.md`
