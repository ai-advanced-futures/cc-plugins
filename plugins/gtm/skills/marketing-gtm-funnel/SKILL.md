# GTM Funnel Design

## Purpose

Design the full customer acquisition funnel with clear stage definitions, measurable metrics, and a motion choice (PLG vs SLG). A funnel without stage definitions is not a funnel — it's hope.

## Stage Definitions

### AARRR Stage Metrics

| Stage | Definition | Key Metric | Aha Moment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acquisition | User discovers product | Unique visitors, Signups | First touch |
| Activation | User gets first value | % activated in 24h | [Define for your product] |
| Retention | User returns | WAU/MAU ratio, D7/D30 retention | Habitual use formed |
| Referral | User invites others | Viral coefficient k | Delight exceeded expectation |
| Revenue | User pays | Conversion %, MRR | Value proven before payment |

### MQL Definition
Marketing Qualified Lead: a contact who has demonstrated intent signals strong enough to be worth sales attention.

Standard signals: downloaded gated content + visited pricing page + email opened 3x in 30 days. Define for your specific motion.

### SQL Definition
Sales Qualified Lead: MQL that sales has reviewed and confirmed matches ICP criteria.

### SAL Definition
Sales Accepted Lead: SQL that has responded to outreach and accepted a meeting.
