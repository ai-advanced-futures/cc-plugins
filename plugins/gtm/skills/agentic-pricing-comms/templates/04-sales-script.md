# Sales Script: Net-New Positioning

**Audience:** Sales · **Vocabulary:** Mixed (positioning + internal deal mechanics)

## Opening Position

"{product} gives you a hard ceiling on spend and a value floor on output.
You know exactly what you pay and exactly what you get — predictable cost,
guaranteed quality assurance."

## Deal Sequence

1. Qualify: Does the prospect value predictable cost over usage-based uncertainty?
2. Demo: Show value floors in action — quality assurance SLA dashboard
3. Close: Present hard ceilings as risk elimination, not a feature

## Internal Deal Notes (Operator Only)

- Amplifier ratio for this segment: {ratio} — do not surface in pitch
- Margin compression floor: {floor} — discount authority requires VP approval
- Vendor margin allocation: {alloc} — finance approval required below threshold
