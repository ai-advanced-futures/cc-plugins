# CEO Readiness Assessment

## Purpose

Evaluate whether a candidate is ready to become a CEO through the **3 A's framework**:
- **Attitude**: Growth mindset, resilience, self-awareness
- **Aptitude**: Strategic thinking, decision-making, leadership capability
- **Action**: Demonstrated results, execution track record

This assessment provides structured evaluation across multiple contexts to inform CEO succession decisions.

## Overview

The CEO Readiness Assessment evaluates candidates through five distinct contexts:

1. **Self-Assessment** - Candidate's own evaluation of readiness
2. **Proxy Assessment** - Evaluation by current/former CEO mentor
3. **Team Assessment** - Evaluation by direct reports and peers
4. **Board Assessment** - Evaluation by board members/advisors
5. **Profile Assessment** - Objective analysis of background/experience

Each context provides unique insights. Combined analysis reveals alignment or gaps between self-perception, organizational view, and objective qualifications.

## Assessment Contexts

### 1. Self-Assessment
**Purpose**: Understand candidate's self-awareness and perceived readiness

**Key Questions**:
- Why do you want to be CEO?
- What are your key strengths for the CEO role?
- What are your development areas?
- How do you handle failure/setbacks?
- What's your vision for the company?
- What makes you ready now vs. 1-2 years ago?

**Evaluation Focus**:
- Growth mindset indicators
- Self-awareness depth
- Motivation alignment
- Realistic assessment of gaps

### 2. Proxy Assessment
**Purpose**: Leverage experienced CEO perspective on candidate readiness

**Key Questions**:
- How long have you mentored/worked with this candidate?
- What CEO-critical situations have they navigated?
- How do they handle pressure/ambiguity?
- What's their strategic thinking capability?
- Where do they need more development?
- Would you recommend them for CEO role?

**Evaluation Focus**:
- Pattern recognition from CEO experience
- Observed growth trajectory
- Readiness for CEO-specific challenges
- Development recommendations

### 3. Team Assessment
**Purpose**: Evaluate leadership effectiveness from those who work closely

**Key Questions**:
- How effectively do they lead your team/function?
- How do they handle difficult decisions?
- How well do they develop talent?
- How do they respond to feedback?
- Do they think strategically beyond their function?
- Would you follow them as CEO?

**Evaluation Focus**:
- Day-to-day leadership effectiveness
- Cross-functional influence
- People development track record
- Team confidence in leadership

### 4. Board Assessment
**Purpose**: Evaluate readiness from governance/strategic oversight perspective

**Key Questions**:
- How well do they understand the business holistically?
- How effectively do they communicate strategy?
- How do they handle board-level questions?
- What's their external stakeholder management capability?
- Do they demonstrate CEO-level judgment?
- What concerns do you have about their readiness?

**Evaluation Focus**:
- Strategic depth beyond functional expertise
- Board-ready communication
- Stakeholder management capability
- Risk awareness and judgment

### 5. Profile Assessment
**Purpose**: Objective analysis of background, experience, and qualifications

**Analysis Areas**:
- **Career Progression**: Rate of advancement, scope growth
- **Functional Breadth**: Exposure to different business areas
- **P&L Experience**: Revenue/budget responsibility size
- **Leadership Scope**: Team size, organizational complexity
- **Industry Knowledge**: Years in industry, sector expertise
- **External Credibility**: Board roles, speaking, publications
- **Education/Development**: Formal qualifications, executive education

**Evaluation Focus**:
- Objective qualification benchmarks
- Experience gaps vs. CEO requirements
- Comparisons to successful CEO profiles
- Timeline to close experience gaps

## Assessment Components

### 1. Growth Mindset Evaluation
**Framework**: Carol Dweck's Growth vs. Fixed Mindset

**Indicators**:
- **Learning Orientation**: Seeks feedback, learns from failures
- **Challenge Response**: Embraces difficult situations vs. avoids them
- **Resilience**: Bounces back from setbacks stronger
- **Development Focus**: Actively works on weaknesses
- **Continuous Improvement**: Always seeking to grow

**Red Flags**:
- Blames external factors for failures
- Defensive about weaknesses
- Avoids challenging situations
- Stopped actively learning/developing
- Fixed view of capabilities

### 2. Attitude Assessment
**Focus**: Character, values, interpersonal effectiveness

**Dimensions**:
- **Integrity**: Ethical decision-making, trustworthiness
- **Humility**: Self-awareness, openness to feedback
- **Empathy**: Understanding others' perspectives
- **Resilience**: Handling pressure, setbacks
- **Authenticity**: Genuine, consistent behavior

**Evaluation Methods**:
- Behavioral examples from all contexts
- Pattern analysis across situations
- Feedback consistency across evaluators
- Values alignment with organization

### 3. Aptitude Assessment
**Focus**: Cognitive capabilities, strategic thinking, leadership skills

**Dimensions**:
- **Strategic Thinking**: Long-term vision, pattern recognition
- **Decision Quality**: Judgment under uncertainty
- **Business Acumen**: Financial, market, operational understanding
- **Leadership Capability**: Inspiring, developing, aligning teams
- **Communication**: Clarity, persuasiveness, listening

**Evaluation Methods**:
- Case scenarios/simulations
- Analysis of past decisions
- Strategic thinking exercises
- 360-degree feedback on capabilities

### 4. Action Assessment
**Focus**: Execution track record, results delivery

**Dimensions**:
- **Results Delivery**: Track record of achieving goals
- **Execution Excellence**: Turning strategy into action
- **Change Leadership**: Successfully leading transformations
- **Problem Solving**: Navigating complex challenges
- **Accountability**: Owning outcomes, learning from failures

**Evaluation Methods**:
- Historical performance review
- Project/initiative outcomes analysis
- Crisis situation handling
- Stakeholder impact assessment

## Output Artifact

The assessment produces a **CEO Readiness Report** with:

### 1. Executive Summary
- Overall readiness rating (Ready Now / Ready in 6-12mo / Ready in 1-2yr / Not Ready)
- Key strengths for CEO role
- Critical development areas
- Recommended next steps

### 2. 3 A's Analysis
- **Attitude Score & Evidence**: Growth mindset, character, values
- **Aptitude Score & Evidence**: Strategic thinking, leadership, business acumen
- **Action Score & Evidence**: Execution track record, results delivery

### 3. Context Analysis
- Self-assessment summary
- Proxy assessment summary
- Team assessment summary
- Board assessment summary
- Profile assessment summary
- **Alignment Analysis**: Where contexts agree/disagree

### 4. Development Plan
- Priority development areas
- Recommended experiences/exposure
- Timeline to readiness
- Support/mentorship needs

### 5. Decision Recommendation
- CEO appointment recommendation
- Timing considerations
- Risk factors
- Mitigation strategies

## Usage

### Discovery Phase Integration

This skill is used during **Discovery Skill > CEO Candidate Evaluation**:

```bash
# Typical workflow:
1. Discovery Skill identifies CEO succession as priority
2. Discovery captures candidate information
3. CEO Readiness Assessment initiated:
   - Self-assessment interview
   - Proxy assessment interview(s)
   - Team assessment (360 feedback)
   - Board assessment interviews
   - Profile analysis
4. Comprehensive CEO Readiness Report generated
5. Results inform succession timeline and development plan
```

### Standalone Usage

Can also be used independently for:
- Executive development planning
- Succession pipeline evaluation
- Leadership bench strength assessment
- External CEO candidate evaluation
