# Anti-AI Tells — Drafting Discipline

Apply while WRITING. The detector loop exists for text that already exists;
this checklist keeps new text from needing it. Adapted from observed edits of
real AI drafts (names and client context stripped).

## The 13 tells (strip every one before shipping)

1. **The `Name —` em-dash opener** — the #1 Claude fingerprint. Fix: open with
   `Name,` then a line break, then the sentence.
2. **Mid-sentence em-dashes** as the default connector. Fix: use a period,
   comma, colon, or parentheses; reserve "—" for rare, real emphasis. On an
   STE surface, use a period instead of a comma (ADR-008). STE bans a comma
   that joins two clauses when both stand alone.
3. **"Not just X, but Y" and tidy tricolons** as default rhythm. Fix: use
   sparingly; break the pattern so the cadence isn't three-beat every time.
4. **Sentence-initial However/Moreover/Furthermore/Additionally.** Fix: start
   with the subject; fold the logical link in mid-sentence or cut it.
5. **Permission-seeking hedges and apologetic framing** ("a few things you
   could send when you get a chance", "no rush"). Fix: make the request
   directly ("Can you please share the following"); replace apologetic padding
   with a flat value statement ("This will be extremely helpful").
6. **Rationale clause on every ask** ("This is important because…"). Fix: trust
   the reader; cut per-ask rationale (keep "Why this matters" only in formal
   deliverables).
7. **Uniform medium-length sentences**, every paragraph the same shape. Fix:
   vary length hard — a short punch, then a longer build.
8. **Intensity modifiers**: very, really, highly, significantly, deeply. Fix:
   cut the modifier, or replace it with the concrete figure or detail that
   earns the emphasis.
9. **Pat phrases**: "it is worth noting", "plays a crucial role", "in today's
   rapidly evolving", "in the realm of". Fix: delete the throat-clearing and
   state the point directly.
10. **Diminutive flourishes / false modesty** ("even a rough napkin sketch is
    perfect"). Fix: cut; state the ask plainly.
11. **Banned corporate vocabulary**: leverage, seamless, synergy, innovative,
    AI-powered, imperative, non-negotiable. Fix: swap for the plain word (use,
    works together, new, must).
12. **Observational noun-phrases where an action verb belongs.** Fix: rewrite
    with an active verb ("the migration anchors the roadmap", not "the
    migration plays a crucial role in the roadmap").
13. **Paragraph-level over-explanation.** Fix: one idea per sentence; stop when
    the point lands.

**The meta-tell (posture, not words):** AI hedges to be polite; a confident
professional asks directly because they've earned the standing. AI drafts stay
generic; a real author adds specific earned context and reduces the reader's
burden. When in doubt, move from breezy-assistant to confident-professional.

## 30-second pre-send scan

grep for: `—`, `--`, "However,", "Moreover,", "not just", "it is worth",
"crucial", "very ", "leverage", "seamless". Zero hits → ship.

## Guardrail

Apply this checklist only to machine-drafted text (L-027). Text quoted from a
real human is not yours to change, because voice-strip alters the words of the
quoted person.
