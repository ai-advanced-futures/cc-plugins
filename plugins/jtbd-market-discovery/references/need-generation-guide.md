# Customer Need Generation Guide

**Author:** Luis F. Amadeo (Chief Agentic Officer)
**Framework:** ACTION + VARIABLE formula (ODI)
**Purpose:** Systematic generation of 80-100 customer needs from job steps

---

## The ACTION + VARIABLE Formula

### Core Concept

```
CUSTOMER NEED = ACTION (verb) + VARIABLE (noun)
```

**ACTION:** What the customer needs to do (verb)
**VARIABLE:** What the customer needs to act upon (noun)

**Example:**
- ACTION: "Minimize"
- VARIABLE: "Time to identify conflicting absences"
- CUSTOMER NEED: "Minimize time to identify conflicting absences"

## Step-by-Step Need Generation Process

### Step 1: Select a Job Step

Choose one job step from your decomposed job.

**Example:** "Plan the stops" (from Navigation job)

### Step 2: Identify Variables in That Step

Ask: "What things need to be acted upon in this step?"

**Variables identified:**
- Optimal sequence of stops
- Routes to stops
- Open times at stops
- Wait times at stops
- Parking availability
- Total number of stops
- Additional time required
- Stop locations

### Step 3: Select Appropriate ACTION Verbs

Consider which phase the step is in, then choose verbs from that phase's verb library.

**Step phase:** PLAN
**Verbs to consider:** Determine, Define, Prepare, Organize, Prioritize, Sequence, Plan, Coordinate

### Step 4: Combine ACTION + VARIABLE

For each variable, combine with appropriate verbs:

| ACTION | VARIABLE | CUSTOMER NEED |
|--------|----------|---------------|
| Determine | Optimal sequence of stops | Determine optimal sequence of stops |
| Determine | Routes to stops | Determine routes to complete errands |
| Determine | Open times at stops | Determine open times for locations |
| Determine | Wait times at stops | Determine wait times at locations |
| Determine | Parking availability | Determine parking for errands |
| Reduce | Total number of stops | Reduce total number of stops |
| Minimize | Additional time required | Minimize additional time for stops |
| Prioritize | Stop locations | Prioritize which stops are essential |

**Result:** 8 needs from 1 step

### Step 5: Validate Need Quality

Check each need against quality criteria:

- ✅ Starts with action verb
- ✅ Includes specific variable
- ✅ Is outcome-focused (not solution-focused)
- ✅ Is measurable
- ✅ Is stable over time (doesn't mention specific technology)

### Step 6: Repeat for All Job Steps

- 8-15 job steps
- 5-10 needs per step (average)
- Target: 80-100 total needs

4. Calculate preparation time needed before departure

**Job Step 2:** "Plan the stops" (PLAN)

**Variables:** Sequence, Routes, Open times, Wait times, Parking, Total stops, Additional time

**Needs:**
1. Determine optimal sequence of stops

3. Determine open times for locations
4. Determine wait times at locations
5. Determine parking availability for errands
6. Reduce total number of stops needed
7. Minimize additional time required for stops
8. Prioritize which stops are essential

**Job Step 7:** "Travel to destination" (EXECUTE)

**Variables:** Speed, Fuel consumption, Safety, Route adherence, Travel time, Comfort

**Needs:**

2. Reduce fuel consumption during travel
3. Ensure safety throughout journey
4. Maintain adherence to planned route
5. Avoid traffic congestion and delays
6. Maximize comfort during travel
7. Minimize stress during driving

**Job Step 9:** "Assess when destination will be reached" (ASSESS)

**Variables:** Arrival time, Delays encountered, Progress against plan, On-time likelihood

**Needs:**
1. Determine accurate arrival time estimate
2. Detect delays affecting schedule
3. Monitor progress against original plan
4. Assess likelihood of arriving on time
5. Verify assumptions about travel duration

**Variables:** Route changes, Alternative routes, Delay mitigation, Recalculation time

## Advanced Techniques

### Technique 1: Vary ACTION Verbs

Don't use the same verb for every need. Vary to capture different dimensions:

**Variable:** "Coverage gaps"

**Various ACTIONs:**
- **Minimize** likelihood of coverage gaps (reduce chance)
- **Detect** coverage gaps early (find them)
- **Correct** coverage gaps quickly (fix them)
- **Avoid** coverage gaps during critical periods (prevent at key times)
- **Monitor** for coverage gaps continuously (watch for them)

**Result:** 5 different needs for same variable

### Technique 2: Add Contextual Qualifiers

Make needs more specific by adding "when" or "during" clauses:

**Basic Need:** "Minimize time to approve requests"
**Enhanced:** "Minimize time to approve requests **during peak absence periods**"

**Basic Need:** "Determine alternative routes"
**Enhanced:** "Determine alternative routes **when traffic congestion is detected**"

### Technique 3: Target Different Performance Dimensions

For each variable, consider multiple dimensions:

**Variable:** "Status updates"

**Dimensions:**
- **Speed:** Minimize time to complete status updates
- **Effort:** Reduce manual effort required for updates
- **Quality:** Ensure accuracy of status information
- **Frequency:** Determine optimal frequency of updates
- **Completeness:** Maximize completeness of status reports

**Result:** 5 needs covering different performance aspects

### Technique 4: Consider Different Stakeholders

Same variable, different stakeholders:

**Variable:** "Project status"

**Stakeholder perspectives:**
- **Team member:** Minimize time to update project status
- **Manager:** Determine status of all projects at a glance
- **Executive:** Verify projects are on track for delivery
- **Client:** Confirm project milestones are being met

**Result:** 4 needs for different roles

## Quality Criteria for Customer Needs

### ✅ Good Customer Needs

**Characteristics:**
- Start with action verb
- Include specific variable
- Are outcome-focused
- Are measurable
- Are stable over time
- Are NOT solution-specific

**Examples:**
- "Minimize time to identify conflicting absences"
- "Determine alternative routes during congestion"
- "Reduce likelihood of coverage gaps during critical periods"
- "Verify all critical tasks are covered adequately"

### ❌ Bad Customer Needs

**Common mistakes:**

**Mistake 1: Solution-Focused**
- ❌ "Use AI to predict absences"
- ✅ "Determine likelihood of future absences"

**Mistake 2: Not Measurable**
- ❌ "Have a good approval process"
- ✅ "Minimize time to approve simple requests"

**Mistake 3: Too Vague**
- ❌ "Manage projects better"
- ✅ "Maintain visibility into project progress"

**Mistake 4: Technology-Specific**
- ❌ "Get Slack notifications about conflicts"
- ✅ "Detect scheduling conflicts proactively"

**Mistake 5: Feature, Not Need**
- ❌ "See a dashboard with charts"
- ✅ "Assess project status at a glance"

## Tips for Generating 80-100 Needs

### Tip 1: Start Broad, Then Expand

First pass: 3-5 needs per step (30-60 total)
Second pass: Add more needs by varying verbs and adding context (60-80 total)
Third pass: Consider different stakeholders and dimensions (80-100+ total)

### Tip 2: Don't Self-Censor

Generate more needs than you need. You'll filter later using opportunity scores. Better to have 120 needs and prioritize than to have 50 and miss opportunities.

### Tip 3: Use the Verb Library

Keep the verb library open while generating needs. Scan for verbs you haven't used yet.

### Tip 4: Think About Pain Points

Where do customers struggle today? What causes frustration? These pain points often reveal high-opportunity needs.

### Tip 5: Consider Different Performance Metrics

For each step, consider: time, cost, quality, risk, effort. Can you generate needs for each dimension?

## From Needs to Opportunities

Once you have 80-100 needs:

1. **Survey customers** for Importance (1-10) and Satisfaction (1-10)
2. **Calculate opportunity** = Importance + max(Importance - Satisfaction, 0)
3. **Prioritize** needs with highest opportunity scores
4. **Build solutions** that address underserved needs (high importance, low satisfaction)

**The needs you generate become the foundation for data-driven opportunity identification.**

## Summary

**ACTION + VARIABLE Formula:**
- Customer Need = ACTION (verb) + VARIABLE (noun)
- Use phase-appropriate verbs (UNDERSTAND, PLAN, EXECUTE, ASSESS, REVISE, CONCLUDE)
- Target 5-10 needs per job step
- Total goal: 80-100 needs across all steps

**Why It Works:**
- Systematic and comprehensive
- Based on how customers actually do the job (job steps)
- Reveals needs customers may not articulate
- Stable over time (not technology-specific)
- Measurable (can survey importance/satisfaction)
