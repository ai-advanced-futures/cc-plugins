## Overview

The **Forces Diagram** helps you understand why customers switch from one solution to another (or why they don't switch). It's complementary to ODI's opportunity scoring.

**Key Insight:**
Even if your solution addresses high-opportunity needs (ODI), customers won't switch unless the switching forces are strong enough.

---

## The Four Forces

```
         ┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
         │                                     │
         │     PUSH of the Situation           │  Forces FOR switching
         │     (Problems with current)         │
         │              +                      │
         │     PULL of the New Solution        │
         │     (Attraction to new)             │
         │                                     │
         ├─────────────────────────────────────┤
         │                                     │
         │     ANXIETY about New Solution      │  Forces AGAINST switching
         │     (Concerns about switching)      │
         │              +                      │
         │     HABIT of the Present            │
         │     (Comfort with current)          │
         │                                     │
         └─────────────────────────────────────┘

         Switch happens when: PUSH + PULL > ANXIETY + HABIT
```

## Forces Balance Analysis

### Force Calculation

| Force | Total Strength | Weight |
|-------|----------------|--------|
| **PUSH** (problems with current) | _____ / 10 | FOR switch |
| **PULL** (attraction to new) | _____ / 10 | FOR switch |
| **ANXIETY** (concerns about new) | _____ / 10 | AGAINST switch |
| **HABIT** (comfort with current) | _____ / 10 | AGAINST switch |

**Switch Likelihood Formula:**
```
Switch Likelihood = (PUSH + PULL) - (ANXIETY + HABIT)
```

**Interpretation:**

| Score | Interpretation | Recommendation |
|-------|----------------|----------------|

| **+5 to +10** | Likely to switch | Good opportunity, reduce anxiety if possible |

| **-5 to 0** | Unlikely to switch | Significant work needed on forces |
| **-10 to -5** | Very unlikely to switch | Re-evaluate solution or target different segment |

## Customer Interview Guide

### Interview Script for Forces

**Introduction:**
"I'm studying how people decide whether to switch from [CURRENT SOLUTION] to [NEW SOLUTION] for [JOB]. I'd like to understand the factors in your decision."

### Questions by Force

**PUSH Questions:**
1. "What problems do you experience with [CURRENT SOLUTION]?"
2. "What frustrates you most about how you [JOB] today?"
3. "If you could wave a magic wand, what would you fix?"
4. "How much time/money do these problems cost you?"
5. "On a scale of 1-10, how urgent is it to solve these problems?"

**PULL Questions:**
1. "What interests you about [NEW SOLUTION]?"
2. "What would it enable you to do that you can't do today?"
3. "How would [NEW SOLUTION] improve your ability to [JOB]?"
4. "On a scale of 1-10, how appealing is [NEW SOLUTION]?"
5. "What specific features are most valuable to you?"

**ANXIETY Questions:**
1. "What concerns do you have about switching to [NEW SOLUTION]?"
2. "What could go wrong if you switched?"
3. "What would you need to see/know before you'd feel confident switching?"
4. "On a scale of 1-10, how risky does switching feel?"
5. "What have you heard from others about [NEW SOLUTION]?"

**HABIT Questions:**
1. "How long have you used [CURRENT SOLUTION]?"
2. "How integrated is it into your workflow?"
3. "What would you need to change if you switched?"
4. "Who else would be affected by a switch?"
5. "On a scale of 1-10, how comfortable are you with [CURRENT SOLUTION]?"

## Example: Navigation App Switching

### Context
- **Job:** "Get to destinations on time"
- **Current Solution:** Paper maps + asking for directions
- **New Solution:** GPS navigation app (circa 2010)
- **Segment:** Daily commuters in urban areas

### Forces Analysis

**PUSH Forces (7/10):**
- Getting lost frequently (severity: 9/10)
- Time wasted finding routes (severity: 8/10)
- Difficulty planning multi-stop trips (severity: 6/10)
- Outdated map information (severity: 5/10)

**PULL Forces (9/10):**
- Real-time traffic updates (appeal: 10/10, high ODI opportunity)
- Automatic rerouting (appeal: 9/10, high ODI opportunity)
- Multi-stop optimization (appeal: 8/10, high ODI opportunity)
- Always up-to-date (appeal: 8/10)

**ANXIETY Forces (4/10):**
- Cost of smartphone + data plan (severity: 7/10)
- Learning curve (severity: 3/10)
- Battery drain concerns (severity: 5/10)
- Privacy concerns (severity: 4/10)

**HABIT Forces (3/10):**
- Familiarity with paper maps (strength: 4/10)
- Already own map books (strength: 3/10)
- Pride in navigating without tech (strength: 2/10)

**Switch Likelihood:** (7 + 9) - (4 + 3) = **+9 (Very likely to switch)**

**Strategy:** Emphasize real-time traffic (strongest PULL + high ODI opportunity), offer free trial to reduce ANXIETY about cost, provide easy onboarding to overcome HABIT.
