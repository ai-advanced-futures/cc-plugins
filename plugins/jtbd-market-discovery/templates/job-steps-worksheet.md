---

## Overview

Once you have a job statement, you need to decompose it into **job steps**. These steps become the foundation for generating 80-100 customer needs.

**Target:** 8-15 steps total across 6 phases
**Result:** Comprehensive coverage of the entire job lifecycle

---

## The 6 Universal Phases

All jobs follow these phases in sequence:

| Phase | Purpose | Common Verbs | Typical Steps |
|-------|---------|--------------|---------------|
| **UNDERSTAND** | Gather information needed | Determine, Identify, Assess, Estimate, Compare | 2-3 |
| **PLAN** | Prepare for execution | Define, Establish, Prepare, Organize, Schedule | 2-3 |
| **EXECUTE** | Perform core activities | Minimize, Maximize, Ensure, Avoid, Maintain | 3-5 |
| **ASSESS** | Evaluate progress | Measure, Verify, Monitor, Track, Validate | 1-2 |
| **REVISE** | Make adjustments | Correct, Adjust, Optimize, Improve, Update | 1-2 |
| **CONCLUDE** | Finalize and close | Complete, Document, Store, Archive, Summarize | 1-2 |

## Step Validation Checklist

For each step you've identified, verify:

| Criterion | Description | ✓/✗ |
|-----------|-------------|-----|
| **Specific** | Not vague or general | |
| **Actionable** | Describes something that can be done | |
| **Sequential** | Follows logical order | |
| **Necessary** | Required for the job (not optional) | |
| **Phase-appropriate** | Fits the phase it's in | |
| **Solution-agnostic** | Doesn't reference specific tools/products | |
| **Consistent granularity** | Same level of detail as other steps | |

## Common Anti-Patterns to Avoid

### ❌ Anti-Pattern 1: Solution-Focused Steps
**Bad:** "Use GPS app to find route"
**Good:** "Determine optimal route to destination"

**Why:** Solutions change (GPS → paper maps → carrier pigeons), needs don't.

---

### ❌ Anti-Pattern 2: Vague Steps
**Bad:** "Do research"
**Good:** "Identify available options that meet requirements"

**Why:** Vague steps don't help generate specific customer needs.

---

### ❌ Anti-Pattern 3: Skipping Phases
**Bad:** Only listing EXECUTE steps, ignoring other phases

**Why:** Jobs are comprehensive. Missing phases means missing customer needs.

---

### ❌ Anti-Pattern 4: Too Granular
**Bad:** "Open vehicle door, Insert key, Turn key, Press brake..."
**Good:** "Prepare the vehicle"

**Why:** Too granular makes the job unmanageable. Aim for 8-15 steps, not 50+.

---

### ❌ Anti-Pattern 5: Steps Out of Order
**Bad:** Putting "Assess results" before "Execute actions"

**Why:** Steps should follow the natural sequence of how the job is performed.

## What Happens Next?

Once you have validated job steps, you'll:

1. **Generate Customer Needs** (see need-generation-guide.md)
   - For each step, identify VARIABLES
   - Combine with ACTIONS to create needs
   - Target: 5-10 needs per step = 80-100 total needs

2. **Create Survey** (see needs-survey-template.csv)
   - List all customer needs
   - Ask importance (1-10) and satisfaction (1-10)
   - Survey 30+ customers

3. **Calculate Opportunity Scores** (use opportunity_scorer.py)
   - Formula: Importance + max(Importance - Satisfaction, 0)
   - Identify underserved needs (15-20 = extremely high opportunity)

4. **Prioritize Innovation** (use full ODI workflow)
   - Focus on high-opportunity needs
   - Design solutions that address those needs
   - Validate with Economic Value to Customer (EVC)
