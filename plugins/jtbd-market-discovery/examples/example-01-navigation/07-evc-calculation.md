# Economic Value Calculation: Navigation App

## EVC Framework

Economic Value to Customer (EVC) quantifies the total dollar value created by solving customer needs.

**Components:**
1. Time Savings
2. Error Reduction  
3. Revenue Increase
4. Risk Reduction
5. Performance Premium

---

## Component 1: Time Savings

### Planning Time Saved

**Current State (from Time-Task Analysis):**
- Checking multiple sources: 5 min/trip
- Estimating departure time: 8 min/trip
- Second-guessing and anxiety: 3 min/trip
- **Total:** 16 min/trip planning time

**With Solution:**
- One-tap departure time: 1 min/trip
- Trusted estimates (no second-guessing): 0 min additional
- **Total:** 1 min/trip

**Time Saved:** 15 min/trip × 30 trips/month = **7.5 hours/month**

**Value:** 7.5 hours × $50/hour = **$375/month**

### En-Route Time Saved

**Current State:**
- Stuck in avoidable traffic: 10 min/trip (20% of trips)
- = 2 min/trip average
- 30 trips × 2 min = 60 min/month

**With Solution:**
- Proactive reroutes avoid 80% of delays
- Remaining delays: 12 min/month

**Time Saved:** 48 min/month = **0.8 hours/month**

**Value:** 0.8 hours × $50/hour = **$40/month**

### Total Time Savings: $415/month

---

## Component 2: Error Reduction

### Late Arrivals Avoided

**Current State (from Time-Task Analysis):**
- Late 6 times/month (20% error rate)
- Cost per late arrival:
  - Direct time: 20 min rushing/apologizing
  - Indirect: missed opportunities, stress, reputation
  - **Estimated cost:** $25/occurrence

**With Solution:**
- Late 1 time/month (97% on-time rate)
- 5 late arrivals avoided/month

**Error Cost Avoided:** 5 × $25 = **$125/month**

### Wrong Route Choices Avoided

**Current State:**
- Sub-optimal route 25% of time
- Adds 8 minutes average when wrong
- 30 trips × 25% × 8 min = 60 min/month wasted
- Cost: (60/60) × $50 = $50/month

**With Solution:**
- Optimal route 90% of time
- Only 3 trips × 8 min = 24 min/month wasted
- Cost: $20/month

**Error Cost Avoided:** $30/month

### Total Error Reduction: $155/month

---

## Component 3: Revenue Increase

### Fuel Savings

**Current State:**
- Sub-optimal routes waste fuel
- Extra 2 miles/week from poor routing
- 8 miles/month × $0.60/mile = $5/month

**With Solution:**
- Optimal routes minimize distance
- Fuel savings: **$5/month**

### Opportunity Value (Arriving On Time)

**Current State:**
- 6 late arrivals/month miss opportunities
- Examples: Lost client meeting, missed pickup (Uber expense), late to interview
- Estimated opportunity cost: $20/month

**With Solution:**
- Only 1 late arrival/month
- Opportunity value captured: **$20/month**

### Total Revenue Increase: $25/month

---

## Component 4: Risk Reduction

### Accident Risk Reduction

**Current State:**
- Rushing when running late increases accident risk
- 6 rushed trips/month at elevated risk
- Accident probability: 0.001 per rushed trip
- Cost per accident: $5,000 (deductible + premium increase)
- Monthly risk: 6 × 0.001 × $5,000 = $30/month

**With Solution:**
- 1 rushed trip/month
- Monthly risk: 1 × 0.001 × $5,000 = $5/month

**Risk Reduction:** $25/month

### Stress-Related Health Costs

**Current State:**
- Chronic travel anxiety and stress
- Estimated health impact: $10/month

**With Solution:**
- Confidence and calm
- Health cost avoided: **$10/month**

### Total Risk Reduction: $35/month

---

## Component 5: Performance Premium

**Base EVC (Components 1-4):**
- Time Savings: $415
- Error Reduction: $155
- Revenue Increase: $25
- Risk Reduction: $35
- **Subtotal: $630/month**

**Performance Differentiation Level:** Medium (15-25% premium)

**Rationale:**
- Significant improvements in time accuracy vs free apps
- Not breakthrough innovation (still using similar interface)
- Solid 15-25% premium justified

**Premium Percentage:** 20% (midpoint)

**Performance Premium:** $630 × 20% = **$126/month**

## Segment-Specific EVC Analysis

### Crisis Avoiders (30%)

**Multiplier:** 1.8x (higher stakes, more frequent high-importance trips)

**EVC:**
- Monthly: $756 × 1.8 = **$1,361/month**
- Annual: **$16,332/year**

**Value Breakdown:**
- Time savings: $747/month (much more planning anxiety)
- Error reduction: $279/month (late arrivals more costly)
- Revenue: $45/month
- Risk: $63/month (more rushing)
- Premium: $227/month

**Optimal WTP Range (20-30% of EVC):**
- $272-$408/month ($3,264-$4,900/year)

**Realistic B2C Pricing:**
- $80-100/month ($960-$1,200/year)
- Represents 21-26% of EVC
- **ROI for customer: 14-17x**

---

### Balanced Users (35%)

**Multiplier:** 1.0x (baseline from analysis)

**EVC:**
- Monthly: **$756/month**
- Annual: **$9,072/year**

**Optimal WTP Range (20-30%):**
- $151-$227/month ($1,814-$2,722/year)

**Realistic B2C Pricing:**
- $50-65/month ($600-$780/year)
- Represents 20-26% of EVC
- **ROI for customer: 12-15x**

---

### Satisfied Users (35%)

**Multiplier:** 0.4x (low stakes, infrequent high-importance trips)

**EVC:**
- Monthly: $756 × 0.4 = **$302/month**
- Annual: **$3,624/year**

**Optimal WTP Range (20-30%):**
- $60-$91/month ($726-$1,087/year)

**Realistic B2C Pricing:**
- $20-30/month ($240-$360/year)
- Represents 20-30% of EVC
- **ROI for customer: 10-15x**

**Strategic Note:** Don't target this segment with premium pricing. Let them use freemium tier.

## Pricing Strategy Recommendations

### Recommended Pricing Tiers

**1. Premium Tier: $69/month ($828/year)**
- Target: Crisis Avoiders + Balanced Users
- Features: Full accuracy suite, unlimited trips
- Positioning: "Never miss an important meeting again"
- Value: $756/month EVC (baseline) → **11x ROI**

**2. Annual Discount: $749/year ($62/month)**
- Save $79 vs monthly (10% discount)
- Encourage annual commitment
- Higher LTV, lower churn

**3. Freemium Tier: $0/month**
- Limited features (basic navigation only)
- Convert Satisfied Users over time
- Viral growth channel

## Value Capture Analysis

**Premium Tier ($69/month):**
- Baseline EVC: $756/month
- Value captured: $69 / $756 = **9.1% of EVC**
- Customer keeps: 90.9% of value = $687/month

**Assessment:** Under-capturing value
- Standard range: 20-30% of EVC
- Could price higher ($150-$227/month)
- But: B2C pricing psychology + competitive pressure

**Strategic Rationale for Under-Pricing:**
1. Market penetration (vs free alternatives)
2. Viral growth and network effects
3. Land-and-expand (upsell Crisis Avoiders later)
4. Competitive moat building
