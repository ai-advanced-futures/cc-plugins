# Job Steps: Get to Destinations on Time

## Universal Job Steps Framework

The job "Get to destinations on time" breaks down into 12 steps across 6 universal phases.

These steps follow the Universal Job Map (Ulwick), adapted for navigation:

**UNDERSTAND → PLAN → EXECUTE → ASSESS → REVISE → CONCLUDE**

---

## PHASE 1: UNDERSTAND (Define the job parameters)

### Step 1: Determine where I need to go

**What happens:**
- Identify exact destination address or location
- Confirm destination details (building, floor, room)
- Understand parking/entry requirements
- Verify location is correct

**Current pain points:**
- Ambiguous addresses ("123 Main St" - which city?)
- Multiple locations with same name
- Incorrect GPS coordinates
- Hidden entrance/parking locations

**Time required:** 2-5 minutes per trip

**Example scenarios:**
- "Meeting at Google office" - which campus? which building?
- "123 Oak Street" - need full address with ZIP
- "John's house" - need to look up in contacts

---

### Step 2: Determine when I need to arrive

**What happens:**
- Check calendar/invitation for arrival time
- Understand buffer requirements (early arrival expected?)
- Account for setup time (parking, walking, finding room)
- Factor in social norms (fashionably late vs punctual)

**Current pain points:**
- Unclear expectations ("meeting at 2pm" - be there at 2 or ready to start at 2?)
- Buffer time ambiguity (how early is safe?)
- Cultural differences in punctuality expectations

**Time required:** 1-2 minutes per trip

**Example scenarios:**
- Job interview: arrive 10 min early
- Doctor appointment: arrive 15 min early for paperwork
- Dinner party: arrive 5-10 min late (social norm)
- Flight: arrive 2 hours early (TSA requirement)

---

### Step 3: Assess current location and constraints

**What happens:**
- Confirm starting point (home, office, current location)
- Check current time vs required departure
- Assess personal constraints (need bathroom break, grab coffee?)
- Evaluate mode of transport options (drive, transit, rideshare)

**Current pain points:**
- Forgetting items (need to go back home)
- Underestimating personal prep time
- Not accounting for parking time
- Unexpected starting location issues

**Time required:** 2-3 minutes per trip

**Example scenarios:**
- "Oh, I need my laptop" - adds 10 minutes
- "Should I drive or take Uber?" - decision paralysis
- "I'm not actually home, I'm at the grocery store"

---

## PHASE 2: PLAN (Select the optimal approach)

### Step 4: Identify available route options

**What happens:**
- Query navigation app for routes
- Consider alternative modes (drive vs transit vs bike)
- Think through familiar vs unfamiliar routes
- Identify potential route constraints (tolls, highways, etc.)

**Current pain points:**
- Too many options (paralysis)
- Missing hidden alternatives (local shortcuts)
- App doesn't know personal preferences (avoid highways)
- Toll road trade-offs unclear

**Time required:** 3-5 minutes per trip

**Example scenarios:**
- Highway (faster but stressful) vs side roads (slower but calmer)
- Toll road ($5 saves 8 minutes - worth it?)
- Familiar route (comfortable) vs new route (anxiety)

---

### Step 5: Estimate travel time for each route

**What happens:**
- Review app's time estimates
- Apply personal experience adjustments
- Factor in current traffic conditions
- Account for time-of-day patterns
- Add parking/walking buffer

**Current pain points:**
- Apps underestimate actual time (15-20% error common)
- Don't account for parking search time
- Miss destination-specific delays (security, elevators)
- Ignore personal driving speed
- Traffic predictions often wrong

**Time required:** 5-10 minutes per trip

**Example scenarios:**
- App says "25 minutes" but parking takes 10 more
- "Usually 30 min but it's rush hour, so maybe 45?"
- "It says 20 min but I drive slower, so 25"

---

### Step 6: Select optimal route

**What happens:**
- Choose route based on time, cost, comfort
- Commit to departure time
- Accept trade-offs (time vs money vs stress)
- Set calendar reminder if needed

**Current pain points:**
- Second-guessing the choice
- Anxiety about making wrong decision
- No clear "right answer"
- Can't easily change mind later

**Time required:** 2-3 minutes per trip

**Example scenarios:**
- "I'll take the slower route because I hate highway driving"
- "Toll road is worth it today - important meeting"
- "I'll leave 10 minutes earlier just to be safe"

---

## PHASE 3: EXECUTE (Implement the selected plan)

### Step 7: Navigate to destination using selected route

**What happens:**
- Start navigation app
- Follow turn-by-turn directions
- Stay in correct lanes
- Manage audio (music, podcasts, calls)
- Monitor progress

**Current pain points:**
- Missed turns (late notification or didn't hear)
- Lane confusion (which lane for next turn?)
- Distraction from phone/app
- Audio interruptions (directions cutting off music)
- App says "turn right" but 3 right turns ahead

**Time required:** Full travel duration (20-60 min typical)

**Example scenarios:**
- "Turn right in 500 feet" but can't see street name
- Trying to merge across 3 lanes for next exit
- Phone died, lost navigation mid-trip

---

### Step 8: Monitor real-time traffic conditions

**What happens:**
- Watch for traffic alerts
- Check ETA updates
- Notice if falling behind schedule
- Listen for alternative route suggestions

**Current pain points:**
- Alerts come too late to avoid
- Don't get updates unless actively checking
- ETA changes unexpectedly without explanation
- App doesn't reroute automatically

**Time required:** Continuous during trip (passive)

**Example scenarios:**
- "Accident ahead" - but already in that lane
- ETA jumped from "arrive 2:05" to "arrive 2:18"
- "Heavy traffic on your route" - but no alternative offered

---

### Step 9: Adapt route when conditions change

**What happens:**
- Accept reroute suggestions
- Make manual route changes
- Take exits or turn based on new info
- Recalculate ETA and communicate delays

**Current pain points:**
- Reroute comes too late
- Suggested alternative is worse
- Anxiety about unknown route
- Hard to manually override app
- Need to notify others about delay

**Time required:** 2-5 minutes when needed (10-20% of trips)

**Example scenarios:**
- "Take next exit to avoid traffic" - but exit is right lane, I'm in left
- Manually navigating to gas station, then resuming route
- Calling ahead: "I'll be 15 minutes late"

---

## PHASE 4: ASSESS (Evaluate the job execution)

### Step 10: Verify arrival time accuracy

**What happens:**
- Check actual arrival time vs predicted
- Note if early or late
- Assess prediction accuracy
- Evaluate success/failure

**Current pain points:**
- No record of prediction accuracy
- Can't remember what app originally predicted
- Don't track patterns over time
- No feedback loop to app

**Time required:** 30 seconds per trip

**Example scenarios:**
- "It said 2:05, I arrived at 2:03 - pretty accurate"
- "It said 25 minutes but took 35 - way off"
- "I was 10 minutes late to my interview"

---

### Step 11: Evaluate route efficiency

**What happens:**
- Reflect on route choice
- Note any issues encountered
- Consider if alternative would've been better
- Learn for next time

**Current pain points:**
- No way to know if other route was better
- Can't see historical "what if" analysis
- Don't remember lessons learned
- Same mistakes repeated

**Time required:** 1-2 minutes per trip (if done consciously)

**Example scenarios:**
- "I should've taken the toll road - worth $5"
- "That highway exit was a disaster - avoid next time"
- "Local route was actually faster than highway"

---

## PHASE 5: REVISE (Modify the approach for next time)

### Step 12: Learn from route outcomes for future trips

**What happens:**
- Update mental model of routes
- Adjust personal time buffers
- Note app accuracy patterns
- Build trust (or distrust) in predictions

**Current pain points:**
- No systematic learning
- Insights not captured
- Personal preferences not saved
- Can't share learnings with others

**Time required:** Minimal (happens subconsciously)

**Example scenarios:**
- "Always add 10 minutes to Google's estimate"
- "Never take Highway 101 between 4-6pm"
- "This destination always takes longer than predicted"

---

## PHASE 6: CONCLUDE (Not applicable for navigation job)

**Note:** The navigation job doesn't have a meaningful "conclude" phase like other jobs (e.g., filing taxes has a conclude phase where you store receipts for audit). Each trip is independent.

However, over many trips, patterns emerge:
- Preferred routes learned
- Trusted apps identified
- Personal time buffers calibrated
- Driving habits adapted

---

## Job Step Insights

### Most Time-Consuming Steps:
1. **Step 5: Estimate travel time** (5-10 min) - High cognitive load
2. **Step 7: Navigate to destination** (20-60 min) - Longest duration
3. **Step 4: Identify route options** (3-5 min) - Decision paralysis

### Most Error-Prone Steps:
1. **Step 5: Estimate travel time** (20-30% error rate) - Apps underestimate
2. **Step 9: Adapt route** (decisions made under stress)
3. **Step 7: Navigate** (missed turns, wrong lanes)

### Highest-Pain Steps (by segment):
**Crisis Avoiders:**
- Step 5: Estimate travel time (anxiety about accuracy)
- Step 8: Monitor conditions (fear of unexpected delays)
- Step 9: Adapt route (stress from changes)

**Balanced Users:**
- Step 5: Estimate travel time (want reliability)
- Step 4: Identify routes (want efficiency)

**Satisfied Users:**
- (Low pain overall - current solutions work fine)
