# Customer Needs: Get to Destinations on Time

## Need Generation Methodology

All 85 needs follow the **ACTION + VARIABLE** formula:
- **Direction:** Minimize, Reduce, Avoid, Ensure, Increase, etc.
- **Action:** time, likelihood, effort, etc.
- **Variable:** outcome-based (what, not how)

### Validation Criteria
✅ Outcome-focused (not solution-specific)
✅ Measurable (can be surveyed 1-10)
✅ Stable (won't change in 5 years)
✅ Controllable (solution can impact it)
✅ Distinct (not duplicate of another need)

## Needs Validation Report

All 85 needs validated against 5 criteria:

### ✅ Outcome-Focused
- No solution-specific needs (e.g., NOT "use AI for predictions")
- All describe desired outcomes, not methods

### ✅ Measurable
- Each can be rated on 1-10 scale for importance and satisfaction
- Concrete enough for survey questions

### ✅ Stable
- Needs won't fundamentally change in 5 years
- "Minimize time to estimate departure" stable regardless of technology

### ✅ Controllable
- Navigation solution can directly impact each need
- Within scope of product influence

### ✅ Distinct
- No duplicate needs with different wording
- Each represents unique customer outcome

## Common Pitfalls Avoided

❌ **Solution-focused:** "Use machine learning for predictions"
✅ **Outcome-focused:** "Minimize error in travel time estimation accuracy"

❌ **Too vague:** "Improve navigation experience"
✅ **Specific:** "Minimize likelihood of missing turns due to late notifications"

❌ **Too specific:** "Reduce travel time from 30 to 25 minutes"
✅ **General:** "Minimize time it takes to navigate to destination"

❌ **Duplicate:** "Reduce lateness" vs "Minimize late arrivals"
✅ **Distinct:** Each need has unique meaning
