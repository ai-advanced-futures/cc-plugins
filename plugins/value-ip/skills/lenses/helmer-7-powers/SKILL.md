# Helmer's 7 Powers Analysis Lens

## Purpose

Determine whether the artifact creates genuine, durable structural barriers to competition using Hamilton Helmer's 7 Powers framework from "7 Powers: The Foundations of Business Strategy."

**Core thesis:** A business has a Power if it creates both benefit (higher margins or reduced costs) AND a barrier to competition that prevents competitors from replicating the advantage.

## The Seven Powers

### 1. Scale Economies
Declining unit costs as volume increases.
- **Benefit:** Lower cost
- **Barrier:** Prohibitive volume required to match cost structure
- **Test:** Does this grow cheaper per unit at scale? Do competitors need massive volume to compete?

### 2. Network Effects
Value increases as more users join.
- **Benefit:** Increased product value
- **Barrier:** Unattractive share for entrant (winner-take-most dynamics)
- **Test:** Does each new user make the product more valuable for others? Is the network self-reinforcing?

### 3. Counter-Positioning
New business model incumbents cannot copy without self-harm.
- **Benefit:** Superior value proposition in new model
- **Barrier:** Incumbent collateral damage if they adopt
- **Test:** Would copying this cannibalise an incumbent's existing revenue model?

### 4. Switching Costs
Costs (time, money, risk) when customer leaves.
- **Benefit:** Increased WTP; lower churn
- **Barrier:** Customer reluctance to switch
- **Test:** How costly and painful is it for customers to replace this?

### 5. Branding
Durable attributions of quality or identity.
- **Benefit:** Price premium
- **Barrier:** Years required to build equivalent brand
- **Test:** Is there a premium buyers pay for the brand itself, independent of features?

### 6. Cornered Resource
Preferential access to scarce resource.
- **Benefit:** Production of a superior product
- **Barrier:** Others cannot access the same resource
- **Test:** Is there a unique asset (data, talent, IP, relationship) that cannot be replicated?

### 7. Process Power
Embedded operational superiority from years of refinement.
- **Benefit:** Better product at lower cost
- **Barrier:** Replication requires years (not investment)
- **Test:** Has the organization developed workflows/processes that take years to replicate?

## Analysis Protocol

### Step 1: Power Scan
For each of the 7 powers, assess the artifact:
- Does this artifact create or enable this power? (Y/N)
- Evidence: specific quote or element from the artifact
- Score: 1 (no power) → 5 (strong power)

### Step 2: Barrier Assessment
For powers identified (score ≥ 3):
- Name the specific barrier to competition
- Estimate time for a well-funded competitor to overcome this barrier

### Step 3: Structural Verdict
- Count powers found at score ≥ 3
- 0 powers: WEAK — commodity, no structural differentiation
- 1-2 powers: MODERATE — some defensibility, but fragile
- 3+ powers: STRONG — strong competitive moat

**Note: STRONG/MODERATE/WEAK are Helmer-internal verdicts about power concentration. The system-level PASS/CONDITIONAL/FAIL verdict is calculated by the synthesizer from average scores across all four frameworks.**

### Step 4: Score Output (1-5)
1 = No powers found
2 = Weak evidence of 1 power
3 = Clear 1 power, some evidence of second
4 = 2-3 clear powers
5 = 3+ clear powers with strong barriers
